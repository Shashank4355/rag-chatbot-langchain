# Deleted files and data retention

## Restoring deleted files

Deleted files go to Trash. You can restore them from Trash for 30 days on Free and Team, 90 days on Business, and 180 days on Enterprise. After that they are permanently deleted and support can't recover them.

Only the owner of a file, or a workspace admin, can restore it.

## Restoring an earlier version

Open the file's menu and choose Version history. How far back you can go depends on the plan (see Plans and pricing). Restoring a version creates a new version; nothing is overwritten.

## Closing an account

When a workspace is closed, its files are deleted 60 days later. Backups that contain the workspace are purged within a further 35 days. Admins can export everything from Admin console > Data > Export before closing.
