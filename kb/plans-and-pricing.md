# Plans and pricing

Kitebox has four plans: Free, Team, Business and Enterprise. Prices are per user and in US dollars. Taxes are added at checkout where they apply.

## Price per user

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing (per month) | Minimum users |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 1 |
| Team | $12 | $10 | 3 |
| Business | $24 | $20 | 5 |
| Enterprise | Custom quote | Custom quote | 50 |

Annual billing is paid up front for twelve months. Enterprise pricing is set by the sales team.

## Storage and file limits

| Plan | Storage | Largest single file | Version history |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 5 GB per account | 2 GB | 30 days |
| Team | 1 TB per user, pooled | 100 GB | 180 days |
| Business | 5 TB per user, pooled | 250 GB | 1 year |
| Enterprise | Unlimited | 250 GB | 10 years |

Pooled storage means every user's allowance is added together and shared across the workspace.

## Features by plan

- Link passwords and link expiry dates: Business and Enterprise.
- SAML single sign-on: Business and Enterprise.
- SCIM user provisioning: Enterprise only.
- EU or US data residency: Business and Enterprise.
- Admin audit log: Team keeps 90 days, Business keeps 1 year, Enterprise keeps 3 years.

## Discounts

Registered non-profits get 30% off Team and Business on annual billing. Kitebox does not offer student or education discounts.
