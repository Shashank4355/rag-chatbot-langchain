# Security and compliance

## Encryption

Files are encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher. Kitebox manages the keys. Customer-managed encryption keys are available on Enterprise.

## Certifications

Kitebox has a SOC 2 Type II report and ISO 27001 certification. Customers on paid plans can request the SOC 2 report under a non-disclosure agreement.

## GDPR and HIPAA

A data processing agreement for GDPR is available to every paid plan from Admin console > Legal. Kitebox signs a HIPAA business associate agreement on Enterprise only; don't store protected health information on other plans.

## Data residency

Business and Enterprise workspaces can choose to keep files in the EU (Frankfurt) or the US (Virginia). The region is chosen when the workspace is created and can't be changed afterwards. Free and Team workspaces are stored in the US.
