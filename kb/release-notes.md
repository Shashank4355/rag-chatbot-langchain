# Release notes

## October 2026

- API v2 rate limits raised: Business from 300 to 600 requests per minute, Enterprise from 600 to 1,200.
- The webhook retry window was extended to about 24 hours, with up to 8 retries (previously 5 retries over 6 hours).

## September 2026

- Team plan: largest single file raised from 50 GB to 100 GB.
- Team plan version history extended from 90 to 180 days.

## June 2026

- Invoice payment with 30-day terms added for Business workspaces. It was previously Enterprise only.
