# Mobile apps

The iPhone and iPad app needs iOS 16 or later. The Android app needs Android 10 or later.

## Offline files

Mark a file or folder "Available offline" to keep a copy on the device. Free accounts can make individual files available offline; whole folders offline need a paid plan.

## Camera upload

Camera upload copies new photos and videos to a Camera Uploads folder. It can be limited to Wi-Fi only. On iOS, background upload stops if the app hasn't been opened for several days, because of how iOS limits background work.

## Lost device

A workspace admin can sign a lost phone out and remove its offline files from Admin console > Devices > Remote wipe. Remote wipe is available on Business and Enterprise.
