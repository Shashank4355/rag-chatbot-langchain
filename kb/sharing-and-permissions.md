# Sharing and permissions

## Roles

| Role | Can view | Can comment | Can edit | Can share | Can delete |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Owner | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Editor | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes, unless the owner turns it off | No |
| Commenter | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| Viewer | Yes | No | No | No | No |

## Shared links

Anyone with a shared link can view the file. On Business and Enterprise you can add a password and an expiry date to a link. Admins on those plans can also block links that work for people outside the workspace.

## Transferring ownership

An owner can transfer a file or folder to another member of the same workspace from Share > Advanced > Transfer ownership. Ownership can't be transferred to someone outside the workspace.

## Guests

Team, Business and Enterprise workspaces can invite guests, who are people outside the workspace. Guests don't count as paid users, can only be Commenter or Viewer, and only see what is shared with them directly.
