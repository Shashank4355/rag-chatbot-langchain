# Billing and refunds

## Payment methods

Kitebox accepts Visa, Mastercard and American Express on all paid plans. Business and Enterprise workspaces can also pay by invoice with 30-day terms. Kitebox does not accept PayPal or cryptocurrency.

## Adding and removing users

When you add a user in the middle of a billing period you are charged a prorated amount for the rest of that period. Removing a user stops charges from the next billing period; the current period is not refunded.

## Refunds

- Annual plans: you can cancel within 14 days of the purchase or renewal date for a full refund.
- Monthly plans: payments are not refunded. Cancelling stops the next charge, and the workspace stays on the paid plan until the end of the month you paid for.
- Refunds go back to the original card and take 5 to 10 business days to appear.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, Kitebox retries the card on days 1, 3 and 5. After 7 days without payment the workspace becomes read-only: people can open and download files but can't upload or edit. After 30 days without payment the workspace moves to the Free plan. Files over the Free storage limit are kept for 60 more days and then deleted.

## Invoices and currency

Invoices are available under Admin console > Billing > Invoices. All plans are billed in US dollars; Kitebox does not bill in other currencies.
